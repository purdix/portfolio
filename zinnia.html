Zinnia

Building a design system that scales

Zinnia is Rose Rocket’s design system, rebuilt from the ground up to support product growth, improve accessibility, and move faster as a team. I led the transformation by creating scalable tokens, establishing accessible foundations, and bridging design and code through documentation and close collaboration. The result:

✅ Robust system

Scalable, reusable patterns used across the product.

✅ WCAG 2.0 compliant

Accessible foundations built into our tokens and components.

✅ Faster handoff

Aligned design and code to reduce friction and rework.

The Problem

A system that wasn't 'system-ing'

When I first joined Rose Rocket, the design system wasn’t really a system at all. The Figma file was disorganized, components were scattered and inconsistent, and the product's UI was a Frankenstein of various out-of-the-box libraries. This created a number of painful challenges for our team:

Limited customization*

Rose Rocket often failed to capture key details unique to each customer’s business, limiting their ability to tailor their system.

Inefficient workarounds*

Our Solutions team often resorted to complex workarounds to meet customer needs, introducing frustrating workflow inefficiencies.

Lack of self-service*

Customers struggled to set up their organizations independently, resulting in high support demands and time-consuming configurations.

High development costs

Customizing the product for individual needs demanded substantial development resources, leading to costly and unsustainable roadmaps.

*Based on over 100+ open customer support tickets from our previous software
