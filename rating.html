Introduction

What is Rose Rocket?

Rose Rocket is a cloud-based Transportation Management System (TMS) built for trucking companies and brokerages. It provides essential tools for managing daily operations, including dispatching, customer communication, billing, and driver management.

The Problem

In trucking, shipments are typically rated based on two types of service:

Full truckload (FTL)

A shipper’s freight fills the entire truck.

Less-than-truckload (LTL)

A shipper’s freight only partially fills the truck.

For FTL shipments, rating is relatively straightforward, as companies usually charge a flat rate for the entire truck.

In contrast, LTL shipments are far more complex, with rates fluctuating based on a multitude of factors. Many companies rely on unweildy spreadsheets and tribal knowledge to calculate these rates, resulting in several key challenges:

Difficult to use

Spreadsheets and manual calculations are difficult for users to manage, slowing down processes and requiring extensive onboarding.

Susceptible to human error

Relying on manual entry increases the risk of errors, which can lead to costly mistakes and potential financial loss.

Uncertain profit margins

Current methods don’t consistently ensure profitability, leaving rates susceptible to undercharging and missed revenue opportunities.

Labor-intensive to set up and maintain

Setting up and updating rates is a time-consuming process that requires specialized knowledge, adding to operational strain and inefficiency.

The Objective

Faced with these challenges, our goal was clear:

How might we streamline the rating process so that users are empowered to rate LTL shipments with greater confidence, accuracy and ease?

Our Approach

While our platform included a rating engine, it only supported flat-rate pricing for FTL shipments. This limitation forced a significant portion of our users, who required more complex LTL rating, to rely on spreadsheets or other TMS solutions.

This project set out to bridge that gap by introducing a flexible system that would empower users to make confident pricing decisions and manage the complexities of LTL rating with greater ease.

Phase 1: Research & Design

Defining scope

While we recognized the need to support LTL rating, the specific requirements were still unclear. To refine our scope, we reviewed past customer calls (via Gong), analyzed support tickets (via Hubspot), and consulted with our internal Solutions team to pinpoint exactly what features were needed.

Reflection

There is clearly a strong market demand for customization within the TMS space. Consequently, Object Builder has strategically positioned Rose Rocket to stand out from competitors by enabling:

  • Tailored custom fields to suit unique business needs
  • Custom templates for efficient setup processes
  • Role-based field permissions to protect sensitive information

Beyond the immediate user benefits, these customization tools also give us a competitive advantage by gaining unique insights into user behavior, such as common field types and configurations.

As engagement with Object Builder and our toolkit grows, it will be exciting to leverage these insights to inform and enhance our core product, ensuring our platform stays aligned with users’ evolving needs and keeps Rose Rocket at the forefront of TMS innovation.
